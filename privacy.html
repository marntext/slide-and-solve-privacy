Privacy Policy

Slide & Solve

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Slide & Solve processes. It is short because the app processes very little.

Personal data we collect

None. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number or location. Nothing that identifies you is sent to us.

Your photos

You can turn one of your own photos into a puzzle. The photo you pick never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, not sent to us and not shared with anyone. It stays in the device's memory for that game only and is gone when the game closes.

The app does not read your whole photo library either. On Android the system photo picker is used and only the single file you choose is handed to the app. On iOS permission is requested and you may decline; the game continues with the pictures that ship with it.

Stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • Your total score, rank and the badges you earned
  • Your best move count per difficulty
  • Your sound, timer and language preferences

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes these records.

Advertising

The app shows one banner advert at the bottom of the screen through Google AdMob.

Adverts are requested as non-personalised, so no interest profile of yours is used to select them. Google may still access limited technical data to serve adverts and prevent fraud; the scope of that is defined in Google's own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Advert content is limited to the "general audiences" rating.

Children

The game's content is suitable for children, but it is not listed in a children's category in the stores; it targets a general audience. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and in fact we collect no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the new version is published at this address.
